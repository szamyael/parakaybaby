01 • CHECK THE INFORMATION

What is Technical Editing?

✓

Technical editing is the process of reviewing, organizing, and improving specialized information so that it is accurate, clear, consistent, and easy for the intended audience to understand.

In simple words: check and improve the details of an informational material so people will understand it correctly.

What is the editor really checking?

🔎 Information
🏷️ Terms
📏 Numbers
🧭 Labels
💬 Captions
🖼️ Visuals
🧩 Sequence
🎧 Audio clarity
COMMON MISCONCEPTION

“Making a video look nice” is not the main concern.

The main concern is whether the information is correct, understandable, organized, and appropriate for the audience.

06 • MAKE VISUALS MOVE

What is Animation?

▶

Animation is the process of creating the appearance of movement by changing images, objects, or digital elements over time.

In simple words: animation makes something appear to move.

●
Watch the idea

A ball can appear to bounce, an arrow can move across a diagram, text can appear, a map can zoom, or water droplets can move through a cycle.

10 • PURPOSE, NOT DECORATION

Does the movement help the audience understand?

POOR USE
SCIENCE!↻↯✦↺

Spinning text, flashing backgrounds, bouncing titles, unnecessary transitions, and constantly moving objects can make concentration harder.

BETTER USE
EVAPORATION → CONDENSATION → PRECIPITATION

The animation directly helps the viewer understand the process.

What role do vectors play?

VECTOR GRAPHICS

17 • BE A CRITICAL VIEWER

Questions students should ask when watching

About Technical Editing

  1. Is the information accurate?
  2. Are the terms used correctly?
  3. Are the labels and numbers correct?
  4. Are the visuals consistent with the explanation?
  5. Is the information organized clearly?
  6. Can the intended audience understand it?

About Animation

  1. What is moving?
  2. Why is it moving?
  3. Does the movement help explain something?
  4. Does the animation show a process or sequence?
  5. Does it help the viewer understand the message?
  6. Is the animation distracting?

20 • TAKE IT WITH YOU

The four ideas to remember

TECHNICAL EDITINGCheck and improve the information.
ANIMATIONMake visual elements move.
VECTORCreate clear visual elements.
VIDEO EDITINGArrange the video.
✦

Main idea: In an informational documentary or vlog, visual elements should not be added just because they look attractive. They should have a clear purpose and help the audience understand the intended message.
